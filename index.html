Shipping, Care & Policy

We ship across Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, and all Egyptian governorates. Standard orders dispatch within 24–48 hours. Orders with Custom Gold Plating require 2–3 extra business days as each piece is plated fresh to order.

All our base pieces are crafted from durable, skin-friendly stainless steel. Selecting the Custom Gold Plating (+120 EGP) option adds a rich, protective gold finish tailored to your order—helping resist perfume, sweat, and daily friction even longer.

Inspect your parcel upon delivery! Exchanges for unworn pieces in their original Glowing Gems pink box are accepted within 48 hours of receipt. Custom gold-plated orders are final sale unless damaged in transit.
